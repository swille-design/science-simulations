How do distance and time help us calculate a cart's average speed?

1. Set up your experiment

Release the cart from the same point on the ramp each time. Change the measurement distance to investigate how long the cart takes to travel.

The stopwatch starts when the cart reaches the bottom of the ramp and stops at the finish marker. Calculate average speed yourself: distance ÷ time.

2. Collect your data

Part 1: How fast is the cart?
  1. Set the measurement distance to 100 cm.
  2. Release the cart and click Record Trial when it finishes.
  3. Repeat until you have 5 trials at 100 cm.
  4. Copy your times to your daily paper. Calculate the average speed for each trial, then find the average of your five speeds.
Part 2: What happens when distance changes?
  1. Set the measurement distance to 20 cm and run one trial.
  2. Repeat at 40, 60, 80, and 100 cm, recording each trial.
  3. Copy your data and calculate average speed on your daily paper.
  4. Use your distance and time measurements to construct a distance-time graph on your daily paper.
